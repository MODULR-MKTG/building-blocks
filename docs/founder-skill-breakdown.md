# Skill Breakdown for Founders

Plain-English overview of every skill in this repo — what it does, who it's for, and when to reach for it.

Use this alongside `registry/skills.csv` to find the right skill before diving into a skill folder.

---

## Email Skills

### Operator Snapshot
**Use when:** You're about to get on a call with a founder and want to know how they write before you talk to them. Or you're preparing a cold outreach and want it to match their voice.

Pulls their public email archive, maps their structure and voice patterns, and drafts one sample email in their style. Takes 5-10 minutes to run. Produces a "Voice & Playbook" doc you can share or use internally.

---

### Email Engagement Analysis
**Use when:** A client wants to understand what's actually working in their email program — not gut feel, real numbers.

Pulls send data from their ESP, analyzes opens, clicks, CTOR, subject line patterns, and list growth over time. Produces a clean report you can present to leadership. Works with any major ESP.

---

### Email Auth Check
**Use when:** A prospect or client is having deliverability issues, or you want to run a quick audit before onboarding.

Checks SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records for any domain in about 30 seconds. Read-only — no credentials needed. Returns a pass/fail with explanations for anything broken.

---

### Campaign Postmortem
**Use when:** A campaign just wrapped and you need a clean retro — for your own records, for a client debrief, or to pull forward what worked.

Synthesizes campaign goals, performance data, and team notes into a structured retro: what happened, what worked, what broke, what changes next.

---

## Positioning Skills

### Ten Second Test
**Use when:** You're reviewing a landing page or above-the-fold copy and want to know if someone would know what you offer within 10 seconds of landing.

Scores clarity, identifies the most confusing element, and gives you one fix. Fast. Useful before any launch.

---

### April Dunford Positioning
**Use when:** You're repositioning an offer, entering a new market, or the current pitch isn't landing.

Runs the Obviously Awesome canvas: competitive alternatives, unique attributes, target segment, value, and market category. Takes 30-60 minutes as a workshop or async intake. Outputs a positioning canvas you can use to write copy, build a pitch, or pressure-test existing messaging.

---

### Hormozi Offer Review
**Use when:** You're launching a new offer, reworking a price, or the conversion rate on a current offer is weak.

Reviews the offer through the Grand Slam Offer framework — dream outcome, perceived likelihood, time delay, effort/sacrifice. Flags the weakest lever and gives specific suggestions for sharpening it.

---

## Operations Skills

### Judgment Execution Parser
**Use when:** Your task list is a mess and you're not sure what you should be doing versus delegating versus automating.

Classifies each task as judgment (needs you), execution (can be delegated or automated), or mixed. Tells you where the bottleneck actually is.

---

### Meeting Prep Brief
**Use when:** You have an external call in the next hour or two and you want to walk in prepared.

Pulls calendar context, CRM notes, and public research on the person and company. Returns a one-page brief: who they are, what they care about, what to ask, what to avoid.

---

### Weekly Operator Audit
**Use when:** You're ending the week and want to know where your time actually went and what's worth systematizing.

Reviews the last 7 days of work, surfaces repeated tasks and hidden drag, and recommends the highest-leverage systems to build next. Good input for your weekly planning or team retro.
