# Writing Rules

Hard rules for any MODULR-written content.

## Patterns NEVER to use

### ❌ "X isn't Y, it's Z"
Lazy rhetorical contrast. Always rewrite.
- **Bad:** "The Professor problem isn't bad ideas — it's good ideas buried under too much setup."
- **Fix:** "The Professor usually has good ideas. They're just buried under too much setup."

### ❌ "Not X. Not Y. Just Z" (and variants)
- **Bad:** "Not small brands. Not beginner newsletters. Brands with audiences, budgets, and teams."
- **Fix:** "These are established brands with real audiences and budgets to match."

### ❌ Staccato parallel lists ("Real X. Real Y. Real Z.")
Same problem — artifice dressed up as punch.
- **Bad:** "Real audiences, real budgets, full marketing teams."
- **Fix:** "Real audiences and budgets to match." — fluid, not performed.

## Voice principles
- Lead with what the reader feels, not what MODULR did ("we analyzed…")
- The score is a proxy — lead with what it represents (opens, clicks, trust, revenue)
- People don't care about "we" — they care about the outcome
- Competence play: data and examples over opinions and vibes
- Make the point directly. Don't dress it up.
- Natural flow beats parallel construction every time
