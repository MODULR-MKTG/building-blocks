# Newsletter Format

Default structure for [This Should Have Been an Email](this-should-have-been-an-email.md).

- **Publication name:** *This Should Have Been an Email*
- **Platform:** Beehiiv
- **Signed:** Braden & Jon | MODULR
- **P.S.** = revenue / product CTA
- **Subject line style:** Direct, outcome-focused
  - Examples: "The three mistakes that make people delete your emails"

## Goal arc
One big idea → one opinion → one proof point → one CTA.
