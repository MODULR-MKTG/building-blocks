# Welcome Sequence

4-email Beehiiv welcome series triggered on new subscription.

**Goal:** Build trust, introduce the MODULR framework, convert to audit/client.
**Arc:** Expectations → Origin story → Proof at scale → Offer to help.

## Email 1: Welcome (Day 0) — LIVE
- **Subject:** Welcome to This Should Have Been an Email
- Sets expectations: one big idea/week, no "just checking in" energy
- CTA: reply with the one email you actually look forward to

## Email 2: Origin Story (Day 2)
- **Subject:** I was told to stop writing copy.
- **Preview:** Not as a suggestion.
- Story: told to stop writing → built a system → 22% YoY opens, 5x CTRs across 103M sends
- CTA: try the scorer at write.gomodulr.com → MODULR Writer Tool

## Email 3: The Data (Day 4)
- **Subject:** We scored 133 emails. 86% had the same problem.
- **Preview:** The data was brutal.
- Average MODULR score: 3.8/10
- Top 3 problems: no hook, no proof, no clear action
- CTA: browse the public scoring database at email.gomodulr.com → Email Scoring Database

## Email 4: The Audit Offer (Day 6)
- **Subject:** We'll audit your emails for free. Here's the catch.
- **Preview:** There isn't one, actually.
- Pitches the Free Loom Audit
- CTA: reply "AUDIT"
