# MODULR Marketplace

**Tier:** DIY (in the [DIY DWY DFY Model](diy-dwy-dfy-model.md))
**Format:** Claude plugins and skills shipped through MODULR's marketplace
**Status:** In development

## What it is
The DIY layer of the MODULR product stack. Packages MODULR's frameworks and tooling into Claude plugins / skills the client can run themselves, without engaging MODULR for delivery.

## Why it exists
- Captures clients below the $7K/mo [Email Consulting](email-consulting.md) price point
- Scales MODULR's IP without consuming founder hours
- Builds a pipeline of warm leads who graduate to DWY ([Strategy Workshop and Audit](strategy-workshop-and-audit.md)) or DFY ([Email Consulting](email-consulting.md))
- Strategic moat — MODULR's methodology + voice as runnable tools
