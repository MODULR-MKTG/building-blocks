# Strategy Workshop + Audit

**Price:** $2,000 (one-time)
**Format:** Structured workshop + email program audit
**Role:** The intake product. Converts qualified prospects into [Email Consulting](email-consulting.md) retainer clients.

```
$2K Strategy Workshop + Audit  →  $7K/mo Email Consulting
```

## What the client gets
- Full audit of their email program scored against the [Email Scoring Rubric](../methodology/email-scoring-rubric.md)
- [3 Types of Boring Email](../positioning/3-types-of-boring-email.md) diagnosis
- Strategy session (workshop format) to align on goals, gaps, and what to build
- Clear roadmap of fixes ranked by revenue impact
- Decision-ready proposal for ongoing [Email Consulting](email-consulting.md)

## Why it works as the entry to the retainer
- Lower commitment than a $7K/mo handshake
- Forces alignment on goals and gaps before retainer starts
- Pre-pays the discovery work the consulting engagement would otherwise sink hours into
- Client walks away with a useful asset whether or not they continue

## Sold as alternative to
- Jumping straight into a retainer cold
- A free strategy call (deeper, paid, lower noise)

## Conversion target
Most workshop clients should convert into [Email Consulting](email-consulting.md).
