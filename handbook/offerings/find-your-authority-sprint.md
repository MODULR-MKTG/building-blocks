# Find Your Authority Sprint

**Price:** $1,500 (validation pilots possible at $1K)
**Format:** 6-week self-directed sprint
**Braden's time per client:** 30 minutes (kickoff call only)
**Team time per client:** ~3–4 hours
**Status:** In development; validating with early pilot clients.

## Who it's for
Founders/creators pre-newsletter or early (<6 months). $200K–$1M revenue. Stuck because they haven't identified their authority.

Distinct from the established creator-educator ICP (5K+ subs, $500K+ revenue) — see [Ideal Client Profile](../icp-and-market/ideal-client-profile.md).

## What they get
- Kickoff call with MODULR TEAM (30 min)
- Authority Discovery Workbook (3 exercises — see [Find Your Authority Framework](../methodology/find-your-authority-framework.md))
- MODULR team review + feedback within 48 hrs
- 6 issue outlines with headlines, key points, CTAs
- Welcome sequence framework (3–5 emails)
- Subject line options via MODULR hook methodology
- Client publishes first 3 issues independently weeks 3–5
- MODULR team scores all 3 issues + delivers refinement plan + next 3 outlines

## Why self-directed (not session-heavy)
Founders can't be in 3.5 hrs of execution per client. Self-directed scales — 20+ concurrent clients possible. Framework does the heavy lifting; team provides feedback at checkpoints.

## Where it sits in [Service Stack](service-stack.md)
**Sidecar — earlier-stage product.** Different ICP than the headline retainer (pre-newsletter vs. established).

Upgrade path (when the client matures):
$1.5K FYA Sprint → $2K [Strategy Workshop and Audit](strategy-workshop-and-audit.md) → $7K/mo [Email Consulting](email-consulting.md)

## Economics
- 200 sprints/year = $300K (hits the 2026 target)
- Team cost per engagement ~$150–300
- Strong margin

## Validation metrics
- 2–3 pilot clients closed Q1 2026
- NPS 8+
- 80%+ publish 3+ issues during sprint
- 70%+ still publishing 3 months later
- 30%+ upgrade to [Email Consulting](email-consulting.md) within 6 months
