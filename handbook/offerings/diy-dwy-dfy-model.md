# DIY / DWY / DFY Model

The clean way to think about MODULR's product stack — three tiers of involvement, three matching price points.

```
DIY  → [MODULR Marketplace](modulr-marketplace.md)            Claude plugins + skills
DWY  → [Strategy Workshop and Audit](strategy-workshop-and-audit.md)   $2,000 (one-time)
DFY  → [Email Consulting](email-consulting.md)              $7,000/month  ★ headline
```

## DIY — Do It Yourself
**[MODULR Marketplace](modulr-marketplace.md)** — Claude plugins and skills that ship MODULR's IP (the [MODULR Method](../methodology/modulr-method.md), the [Email Scoring Rubric](../methodology/email-scoring-rubric.md), the [3 Types of Boring Email](../positioning/3-types-of-boring-email.md) diagnosis) as tools the client can run themselves.

- Client owns the work
- MODULR ships the system
- Lowest price, highest reach

## DWY — Done With You
**[Strategy Workshop and Audit](strategy-workshop-and-audit.md)** — $2K one-time.

- Structured workshop + audit of the client's email program
- MODULR brings the diagnosis and the roadmap; the client does the work
- The clean intake path into the retainer

## DFY — Done For You
**[Email Consulting](email-consulting.md)** — $7K/month. **The headline product.**

- Ongoing consulting retainer
- MODULR drives the email system; client owns strategy/voice approval
- Best fit for established founder-led businesses with meaningful revenue at stake

## Sidecar offering (not on the ladder)
**[Find Your Authority Sprint](find-your-authority-sprint.md)** — $1.5K, for early-stage brands building authority who aren't yet ready for the DWY/DFY path.

## Why this framing matters
- One clear shelf for every prospect — no menu confusion
- Each tier has a defensible price gap (~3.5×) so upgrades feel rational
- Maps cleanly to the [Sales Funnel](../sales/sales-funnel.md) and [Service Stack](service-stack.md)
