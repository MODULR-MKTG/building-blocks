# Pricing Anchors

Quick reference for current MODULR pricing. **Headline: $7K/mo [Email Consulting](email-consulting.md) (rack rate). $5K/mo is the consulting floor.**

| Offer | Price | Format | Status |

## Pricing principles
- **$7k rack / $5k floor.** Will set $5k as the consulting floor — don't quote below it.
- Workshop ($2k) credits 1:1 toward the first month's retainer.
- Retainers are month-to-month — no minimum term.
- Don't negotiate on price during sales calls. Drop the price, sit in silence.
- If pushback comes, it's usually positioning/value articulation, not the number.
- For inbound: under-charging is the more common error.
