# Free Tools

Two free tools sit at the top of the funnel. Both put the [MODULR Method](../methodology/modulr-method.md) in a prospect's hands before they ever talk to us — the clearest demonstration of how we think, and the cleanest way into the [Sales Funnel](../sales/sales-funnel.md).

## MODULR Writer
**URL:** write.gomodulr.com

An AI email scorer. Paste an email and get it scored against the [Email Scoring Rubric](../methodology/email-scoring-rubric.md) — every [MODULR Method](../methodology/modulr-method.md) element rated, [boring penalties](../positioning/3-types-of-boring-email.md) flagged, a final score out of 10, and what to fix first.

- Shows the methodology working live, on the reader's own copy
- Lands them on a score they want to improve — the start of every good sales conversation

## Email Scoring Database
**URL:** email.gomodulr.com

A public database of scored emails. Browse real emails run through the rubric and see why each one earns its score.

- Proof the rubric is consistent and repeatable
- A reference library that makes the abstract framework concrete

## How they feed the funnel
Both tools are lead magnets. They're pushed in the [Welcome Sequence](../voice-and-style/welcome-sequence.md) (Emails 2 and 3), used as proof in outreach, and lead into the paid [Strategy Workshop and Audit](strategy-workshop-and-audit.md). The DIY [MODULR Marketplace](modulr-marketplace.md) is the productized, deeper version of the same IP.
