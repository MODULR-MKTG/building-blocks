# Find Your Authority Framework

The framework that powers the [Find Your Authority Sprint](../offerings/find-your-authority-sprint.md) product. Client self-directs through three exercises.

## Authority = intersection of three dimensions
```
         EXPERTISE
        (What you know deeply)
             │
             ├─── AUTHORITY lives here
             │    (the intersection)
    OPINION ─┼─ AUDIENCE
  (Your POV)   (Who cares)
```

## Exercise 1: Authority Audit
Map expertise + opinion + audience to find the intersection.

- **Expertise:** What do people come to you for? What could you talk for 30 min with no notes about? 10,000+ hour topics?
- **Opinion:** What do most people in your industry get wrong? What advice makes you cringe? What's your contrarian take?
- **Audience:** Who specifically benefits from your expertise + opinion? Who would pay for it?

**Output:** Authority Map (one-page visual)

## Exercise 2: Content Territory Definition
Define 3–5 recurring content territories. Each must:
- Connect back to your authority
- Have a clear revenue path
- Together give 50+ weeks of content without repetition
- At least one should be opinion-forward

## Exercise 3: Newsletter Architecture
Decide format, cadence, platform, distribution, and revenue path.
