# Email Scoring Rubric Improvements (planned)

The current [Email Scoring Rubric](email-scoring-rubric.md) is solid; these are the planned enhancements to make it industry-standard.

## High priority
1. **Email type context matrix** — different scoring weights for cold outreach vs. newsletter vs. cart abandon vs. onboarding
2. **Make Revenue Path scored 0–4** (currently a qualifier) — without revenue path, nothing else matters
3. **Severity weighting** — weak CTA or no revenue path is fatal; weak data is recoverable

## Medium priority
4. Add story/narrative qualifier (yes/no)
5. Enhance data scoring with recency + relevance + believability
6. Add real scored examples (Robot ~3/10, Good ~8/10, before/after rewrite)
7. Industry benchmark scores (B2B SaaS, e-commerce, coaching, agency)

## Low priority
8. Tie-breaker rules for boring type classification
9. Advanced optimization guide for high-performers (8+/10)
10. Diagnostic flow chart
