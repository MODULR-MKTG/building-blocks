# Email Scoring Rubric

How MODULR scores every email — the basis for the Free Loom Audit, MODULR Writer Tool, and client deliverables.

## Scoring model
Score each [MODULR Method](modulr-method.md) component **0–2**:
- 2 = strong and effective
- 1 = present but could be stronger
- 0 = missing or very weak

**Total:** 12 points → normalize to /10 → subtract boring penalties.

```
Component score (out of 12) ÷ 12 × 10 = Normalized /10
Normalized − boring penalties = Final score /10
```

## Boring penalties
-1 point each for any [3 Types of Boring Email](../positioning/3-types-of-boring-email.md) detected:
- Robot
- Professor
- Clinger

## Performance bands
- **7–10** Strong — will perform well
- **5–6.9** Adequate — needs improvement in key areas
- **0–4.9** Weak — major overhaul needed

## Revenue Path (qualifier, not scored)
- Direct (clear path to purchase)
- Nurture (moves toward buying decision)
- Engagement (builds relationship for future offers)
- None (warning sign)

## Diagnostic priority (when scoring <18)
1. No revenue path? Fix that first. Nothing else matters.
2. Weak CTA? Fix next — no action = no conversion.
3. Weak hook? Fix next — won't get read.
4. Then optimize Upside and Opinion.
