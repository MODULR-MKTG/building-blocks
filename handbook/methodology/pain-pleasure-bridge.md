# Pain Pleasure Bridge

Sales framework used in MODULR outreach and discovery. Used in Free Loom Audit structure and discovery calls.

```
Pain Island → Pleasure Island → Bridge
```

- **Pain Island** — where the prospect lives today (low conversion, boring emails, revenue leaking)
- **Pleasure Island** — where they want to be (predictable revenue per send, converting at 2%+, clients reply asking to buy)
- **Bridge** — what gets them across (the engagement, the rewrite, the sprint)
