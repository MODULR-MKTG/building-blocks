# 2026 Vision

**Theme:** Abundant Opportunity
**Core idea:** Build a business that creates freedom, not just income.

## The vision in one paragraph
> $300K/year MODULR working 30 focused hrs/week. Energy in evenings, protected Friday family / Saturday date nights, present dad, local friendships, 30 lbs lost, finding/creating/taking/sharing opportunities across every area of life.

## The flywheel
```
MODULR grows → sales/marketing/delivery have clear playbooks
            → 30 focused hrs/wk on MODULR
            → increased energy for family, health, community
            → I feel good → better work output → MODULR grows more efficiently
```

Business success enables life success which enables better business. They're one system.


## Decision filter
Every choice: **Does this create abundant opportunity?**

For business:
- Does this move MODULR toward $300K with 30 hrs/wk?
- Am I taking the opportunity or sitting on it?

For lifestyle:
- Does this give energy or drain me?
- Time for family or stealing it?
- Build community or isolation?
