# Q2 2026 Goals

These are MODULR's goals for the rest of Q2.

Not annual ambition. Not vague intention. What can actually ship by June 30.

## MODULR

**Goal:** Ship MODULR OS v1 by June 30.

### What ships
- Public repo with updated positioning, agent constitution, and Q2 goals
- One usable client portal flow in production
- One operating cadence doc that defines how MODULR runs week to week
- Repo used in at least 3 real sales or partner conversations

### How we'll know it worked
- The repo is live and coherent
- The portal is being used for live client work
- The OS gets referenced in actual conversations, not just published and ignored

## Braden

**Goal:** Make **revenue-first email systems** the category people hear when they hear MODULR.

### What ships
- Top-line messaging updated everywhere that matters
- Public OS language aligned to current direction
- Repo used in outreach, follow-up, and sales conversations
- One real commercial step closed by June 30

### How we'll know it worked
- No major drift between what MODULR is and how it describes itself
- At least one opportunity advances because of the sharper framing
- One next revenue step closes

## Jon

**Goal:** Make one active client engagement the proof that MODULR OS can run delivery.

### What ships
- One active client visible in the portal as the working source of delivery truth
- A repeatable task, ownership, and next-step rhythm proven in live client work
- One documented delivery workflow that can be reused on the next client
- Less ambiguity about who owns what and what happens next

### How we'll know it worked
- Active client work can be understood from the system, not just memory or fragmented tools
- The same rhythm can be reused on the next engagement
- Delivery feels calmer and more legible

## Maude

**Goal:** Build MODULR's intelligence layer v1 so company context and next actions no longer depend on manual routing through Braden.

### What ships
- Agent constitution
- Q2 goals doc
- Operating cadence doc
- Maintained decision-ready context for active clients and expansion opportunities
- Clear weekly synthesis of revenue and delivery state

### How we'll know it worked
- Important context is retrievable without asking Braden
- Fewer dropped threads across active work and pipeline
- Decisions get made from prepared context, not reconstruction
