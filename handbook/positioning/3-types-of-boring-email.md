# 3 Types of Boring Email

The dominant archetypes that wreck email revenue. Branded IP — paired with the [MODULR Method](../methodology/modulr-method.md) scoring rubric. Each gets a -1 boring penalty in the [Email Scoring Rubric](../methodology/email-scoring-rubric.md).

## 🤖 The Robot
- Corporate jargon ("leverage," "optimize," "enterprise-grade")
- All features, zero personality
- Sounds like "Todd from legal wrote it"
- Phrases: "We're excited to announce…"

## 🎓 The Professor
- 800+ words
- Dense expertise, no story
- Needs to prove they're the smartest
- "TL;DR" is the reader's first thought
- Phrases: "A deep dive into emerging paradigms…"

## 📎 The Clinger
- Apologetic, permission-seeking
- Weak hesitant CTAs
- "Just checking in…" energy
- Phrases: "Hope this isn't a bad time!"

## Fixes by type
- **Robot:** add personality, remove jargon, swap a feature bullet for a customer story
- **Professor:** cut in half, then half again; put context at the end
- **Clinger:** replace "just checking in" with "here's what you're missing"; make CTAs commands
