# Three Motions

"Revenue-first email" (the committed premise — see [IP Pyramid](ip-pyramid.md)) applies to every newsletter business, but the *tactics* diverge across three motions. **Identify the motion first. Then prescribe tactics.**

| | **Product** | **Service** | **Sponsorship** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **What the newsletter sells** | A product the business owns (info product, SaaS, ecommerce, course) | Leads/trust for a high-ticket service the business delivers | Inventory (ad slots, mentions) to third-party advertisers |
| **Who pays** | Subscriber → buyer | Subscriber → qualified lead → client | Advertiser (subscriber is the product) |
| **Primary revenue metric** | Revenue per subscriber per month (RPS) | Qualified leads × close rate × contract value | RPM (revenue per 1,000 sends) and renewal rate |
| **Healthy gross margin lever** | Conversion rate × LTV | Lead quality (close rate, not lead volume) | List growth + open/click rate (ad-friendly metrics) |
| **List size matters because…** | More subs = more buyers at the conversion rate | Less than you think — 1 right buyer beats 10k tire-kickers | A lot — advertisers pay on volume |
| **Cadence** | Higher frequency works (more swings = more sales) | Lower frequency, deeper value (every send must earn the relationship) | High frequency normalized; advertisers expect inventory |
| **Examples** | Morning Brew (acquired), Sahil Bloom (course), Justin Welsh (course) | MODULR itself, most B2B consultancies, agencies | Morning Brew (pre-exit), Milk Road, The Hustle |

## How [MODULR Method](../methodology/modulr-method.md) (BMODULR) bends per motion

The six elements stay the same. What changes is **what each element is optimizing for.**

- **Message (hook)** — Product: friction-removing curiosity ("the 2-minute fix that..."). Service: pattern-interrupt about a buyer's worldview. Sponsorship: high-CTR-bait that still feels editorial.
- **Opinion** — Product: defends why this product matters now. Service: defends *how you think* (POV is the entire sales pitch). Sponsorship: defends a take that earns the audience's attention so they tolerate the ad.
- **Data** — Product: outcome data ("X customers got Y"). Service: case-study micro-proof ("this client did Z in 90 days"). Sponsorship: industry-level data the audience can use in their own work.
- **Upside** — Product: vivid before/after. Service: what their business looks like after working with you. Sponsorship: useful takeaway the reader gets *regardless* of clicking the ad.
- **Link (CTA)** — Product: direct purchase or low-friction trial. Service: book a call, reply, or "see if we're a fit." Sponsorship: clear separation between editorial CTA and ad CTA (don't compete).
- **Revenue** — Product: every email should be traceable to a SKU. Service: every email is part of a long pre-qualification arc; revenue is downstream. Sponsorship: revenue is the inventory itself; editorial must protect the open rate that sells the ad.

## How the motions show up in practice

- **MODULR itself** — Service motion (our newsletter sells our consulting)
- **A founder-led education brand** — Service motion (newsletter drives product sales, but the buying motion is service-led)
- **A RevOps/consulting engagement** — Service motion (the newsletter supports the retainer relationship)
- **A SaaS product** — Product motion (newsletter drives trial → upgrade)
- **An equity/partnership newsletter** — often hybrid (product + sponsorship)

## Decision rules

1. **Diagnose the motion before prescribing tactics.** A "frequency" or "CTA" recommendation that's right for product is often wrong for service.
2. **Don't mix motions in one program without intent.** A service-motion newsletter that sells sponsor ads dilutes both. If you must, gate the ad inventory tightly.
3. **Long-term plays still count.** A service-motion newsletter that won't generate revenue for 18 months is revenue-first if the system is built for it. See [IP Pyramid](ip-pyramid.md) for the intent-vs-timeline nuance.
4. **The premise is fixed; the motion is the variable.** "Revenue-first email" never bends. The motion determines what "revenue" means.
