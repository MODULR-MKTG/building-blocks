# Discovery Call Framework

30-minute call structure for a paid engagement.

## Goal
Understand email goals → Show how [MODULR Method](../methodology/modulr-method.md) identifies gaps → Pitch [Strategy Workshop and Audit](../offerings/strategy-workshop-and-audit.md) ($2K) as the entry, with the path to $7K/mo [Email Consulting](../offerings/email-consulting.md) from there.

## Structure (30 min)
| Min | Phase | What happens |
|-----|-------|--------------|
| 0–5 | **Context** | "Tell me about your email program. What's working? What's not?" |
| 5–15 | **Audit walkthrough** | Share screen, show their MODULR scores, explain gaps |
| 15–20 | **Impact** | "If we fixed [X gap], what would that mean for your business?" |
| 20–25 | **Offer** | Pitch the [Strategy Workshop and Audit](../offerings/strategy-workshop-and-audit.md) as the next step (path to [Email Consulting](../offerings/email-consulting.md)) |
| 25–30 | **Next steps** | "Which feels like the right fit?" → send proposal |

## Take control of the call
- Never open with "So, where do you want to start?"
- YOU set the agenda
- Keep asking probing questions until they tell you what they *really* care about
- Then cater the entire pitch to that specific problem

## Don't negotiate on price
At the end, **drop the price → sit in silence**.
- If "wow, expensive": say nothing
- Don't offer a discount
- It is what it is

## Objection handling
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I don't have budget" | "What's the cost of leaving 30% of revenue on the table each month?" |
| "I need to think about it" | "What questions can I answer to make this easier?" |
| "Not sure it'll work" | "Fair. What if we start with one sequence rewrite and measure results first?" |

## If they don't close on the call
Follow up **forever**. Not 1 follow-up. Until explicit "no."
