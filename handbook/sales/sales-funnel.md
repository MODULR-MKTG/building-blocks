# Sales Funnel

The full path from cold to closed at MODULR.

```
Cold prospect
     ↓
Outreach (3-message DM split, Loom-led)  →  [Outreach Playbook](outreach-playbook.md)
     ↓
Discovery call booked                     →  [Discovery Call Framework](discovery-call-framework.md)
     ↓
Proposal sent
     ↓
Engagement starts (workshop or retainer)
```

## Top-of-funnel sources
1. **Personalized outreach** — Audits or warm connection
2. **Inbound from email.gomodulr.com / write.gomodulr.com**
3. **Baby Bathwater** + similar density plays
4. **Referral partners** (consultants, launch strategists, community builders who serve same ICP)
5. **LinkedIn proof-of-work content** (not creator-style audience building — see [Creator vs Consultant Model](../positioning/creator-vs-consultant-model.md))

## Funnel math
- 10 outreaches/wk → 25–30% response → 2–3 audits/wk → ~1–2 closes/mo @ $2–5K
- Annual: 12–24 closes minimum at this volume
