# Outreach Playbook

3-step funnel: **Outreach Message → Book a Call → Sales Call**.

## Bad outreach (0/10)
- Lead with title: "Hey I'm a ghostwriter…" → "Hi, I have something to sell you"
- Admit beginner status: "I'm trying to build my client base"
- Free without context: free flyers on a street corner
- Premature ask: "All I ask is a quick video testimonial"

## Good outreach (Juan Gomez → Dickie Bush, 10/10)
3 messages split across ~20 minutes:
1. Compliment + show you've consumed their work
2. Credibility + crystal-clear offer ("I edit your next 3 videos, free, you decide")
3. Friendly nudge

## Pro tips
- Start conversational
- Break messages apart — feels human, not copy-paste
- Show you're learning from them
- Add credibility (lightly)
- Show shared mission ("I connect with the topics you talk about")
- Make the offer binary — zero thinking required

## MODULR template (subject + body)
**Subject:** "[Specific compliment] (but [specific gap])"

```
Hey [Name],

I run email at I Will Teach You To Be Rich (10M emails/month)
and track ~80 creator brands through a side project I run.

Ran your [sequence type] through our framework and wanted to flag something:

Your [specific email] is [exceptional / world-class / solid].
[Why it's good: specific score, specific elements, specific example]

But [specific gap].
[What's missing, what it costs, specific example]

At IWT, we'd [specific fix].

I recorded a quick 5-min Loom breaking down the full sequence
if you want it. No pitch, just showing you what I'd fix.

Interested?

— Braden
MODULR

P.S. [One more specific callout or compliment]
```

## Booking the call (3 rules)
1. **Follow up until it happens.** 99% of people fail by asking once.
2. **Propose a specific time** — don't put the burden on them.
   > "Tuesday at 3:30pm ET for a quick 30-minute chat?"
3. **Counter the objection + signal value:**
   > "If that doesn't work, let me know a window and I'll do my best to make myself available."

## Diagnosing failure
| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| No response to outreach | Wrong target, or too pitchy | Mid-tier creators w/ visible gaps; rewrite conversational |
| Responds but won't book | Insufficient education | More problem articulation via DM before asking |
| Books but doesn't close | Insufficient education OR no price anchoring | Anchor to cost of inaction |
